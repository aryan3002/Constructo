# Fable 5 prompt — Constructo marketing website

> Hand this whole block to Fable 5. It is self-contained — Fable should NOT crawl the product repo (it contains half-built features + real personal data). Everything it needs to know is in this brief.
> Set `{{BRAND}}` first: the product is "Constructo" internally; the contractor app is branded "Neev · Site Register". Decide your public brand name and replace `{{BRAND}}` throughout (default to "Constructo").

---

You are an elite product designer + front-end engineer. Build a **best-in-class SaaS marketing website** for **{{BRAND}}**, an AI-native construction-management platform for India. Aim for the quality bar of Linear, Vercel, Stripe, and Arc — but with a **warm, grounded, construction-meets-craft** identity, NOT generic tech-SaaS. This must look like a real, funded, design-led company.

## What the product is (the truth — use this, don't invent beyond it)

{{BRAND}} turns the chaos of a construction project — the WhatsApp messages, site photos, voice notes, delivery slips, and material decisions — into **one trustworthy, evidence-anchored record**, and gives every person their own honest view of it: the owner, the site team, the accountant, and the homeowner.

The core idea (lead with this — it's the moat): **AI proposes, a human commits.** The AI does the heavy lifting (capture → structure → surface), but a *named human always commits the truth*, and raw AI never reaches the person who can't sanity-check it. **The metabolism, not the mouth, is the product.** Numbers come from deterministic math, never from a chatbot guessing — every figure is traceable to its evidence.

Two emotional promises the whole product delivers:
- For the **builder/owner:** *"Your 3 decisions for the day, from evidence, before 7:15am."*
- For the **homeowner:** *"Relax — nothing needs you today. Here's your home, honestly."*

It's built for how Indian construction actually runs — on WhatsApp, in voice notes, on-site, in mixed languages — not how a Western SaaS imagines it.

## Audience & goal

A flagship product site. Primary buyer = **construction firms / builders**; it must also build credibility with **homeowners** and **investors**. Primary CTA: **"Book a demo"** / **"Get early access."** Secondary CTA: **"Try the free Site Survey tool."**

## Brand identity & art direction (follow precisely)

Warm, premium, trustworthy, engineered-yet-human. Think: blueprint warmth + calm daylight. **Avoid every generic-AI-SaaS cliché** — no purple/blue gradients, no glowing orbs, no robot/brain imagery, no dark-mode-by-default neon.

- **Palette:**
  - Canvas: warm paper / sand — `#F3EFE6` and `#EFEADF` (never clinical white `#FFFFFF`).
  - Ink (text): `#1B1916`; muted `#7A7368`.
  - Primary accent (energy, CTAs): **marigold `#F0A21F`** (pressed `#D6850C`).
  - Secondary accent (calm, trust): **sage green `#3E7A66`**.
  - Status/semantic (use sparingly): ok `#2F7D52`, warn `#C77A12`, risk `#B23A2E`, info `#3A6491`.
- **Type:** Display/headlines = **Bricolage Grotesque** (bold, characterful). Body/UI = a clean humanist sans (**Inter** or **Mukta**). Numbers, money, data = a **monospace** (e.g. **Spline Sans Mono** / IBM Plex Mono) — echo the product's "every number is real" feel. Indian-rupee figures shown in mono with Indian digit grouping (₹1,20,000).
- **Texture & imagery:** warm, real Indian construction + finished-home photography (or tasteful editorial illustration). Subtle paper grain, hairline rules, soft shadows, generous whitespace, sturdy rounded corners (10–18px). A "register / ledger" motif (folded-corner cards, stamped-record feel) is on-brand.
- **Motion:** purposeful and restrained — scroll-reveal, a living hero product mock, subtle parallax. Never gratuitous.

## Site structure (one long-scroll landing page + the sub-pages noted)

1. **Sticky nav** — wordmark, anchor links (Product, How it works, For builders, For homeowners, Pricing), and a marigold **"Book a demo"** button.
2. **Hero** — a bold one-line headline (e.g. *"Every site decision, from evidence — before 7:15am."*), a one-sentence subhead, primary + secondary CTA, and a striking **product visual** (a warm mock of the owner's morning brief or the three surfaces side by side). Convey trust + calm instantly.
3. **The problem** — the WhatsApp reality: six roles, six groups, six versions of the truth; decisions made from memory and hierarchy, not facts. Make the reader feel the chaos.
4. **The shift** — {{BRAND}}'s thesis: one trustworthy record, fed by AI, committed by humans. The "AI proposes, human commits" principle, shown simply.
5. **How it works** — the loop, in 4 clear steps: **Capture** (voice/photo/chat, the way teams already work) → **Structure** (AI drafts; a human confirms) → **Surface** (the right honest slice for each person) → **Act** (approvals, reconciliation, the homeowner's calm view). A clean horizontal flow diagram, warm and legible.
6. **The three surfaces** — present them as one product with three faces, each with a device mock and one-liner:
   - **The Desk** (contractor web) — the material spec schedule, reconciliation, the command center.
   - **The Site** (contractor mobile) — voice-first, English-first, glove-and-glare capture + the owner's 7:15am brief.
   - **The Glass** (homeowner app) — a calm, honest, room-by-room view; "nothing needs you today."
7. **Why it's trustworthy (the differentiator)** — the honest-AI / deterministic section. Numbers come from math, not a chatbot; every figure links to its evidence; no fake progress %; money is **tracking-only — it never moves money**; the AI never shows the homeowner anything a human hasn't approved. This is the section that separates {{BRAND}} from the hype crowd — make it confident and specific.
8. **The lifecycle** — Day 0 **Site Survey** → **Design & Material Spec** → **Execution & Site Audit** → **Homeowner reassurance**. Show {{BRAND}} as the spine of the whole journey.
9. **Free Site Survey tool** — a callout for the free, public, no-login site-survey tool that generates a professional branded PDF in minutes (the top-of-funnel hook). CTA: "Try it free →". (Build a simple sub-page placeholder for it.)
10. **For each persona** — a compact 4-up: Owner, Site team, Accountant, Homeowner — "what you get" in one line each.
11. **Pricing** (sub-page or section) — keep it honest and simple: **per active site, not per user; supervisor seats free; AI included, not upsold.** Show as "Early-access pricing — talk to us," with an illustrative tier. Do not invent precise numbers as if final.
12. **FAQ** — honest answers (Does it replace WhatsApp? No — it works with it. Is my money moved? No — tracking only. What about Hindi/voice? Built for it. Is the AI making decisions? No — it proposes, you commit.).
13. **Final CTA** — full-width, warm: "Book a demo / Get early access."
14. **Footer** — links, a short honest mission line, contact.

## Copy & tone

Confident, warm, plain-spoken, India-grounded, and **honest** — honesty is the brand. Short punchy headlines. Be specific (the 7:15am moment, "50 bori of cement, traced to the photo"), never vague-corporate. No jargon, no hype words ("revolutionary", "world's first", "fully autonomous"). Write like a sharp founder who respects the reader.

## HARD do-nots

- **Do NOT** crawl or reference the product source repo or any internal docs.
- **Do NOT** use any real people's names, phone numbers, addresses, or the "Tripathi / CivilArch" data — invent neutral placeholder names if examples are needed, and label them as illustrative.
- **Do NOT** fabricate customer logos, testimonials, ratings, user counts, funding, or live metrics. The company is pre-launch — credibility comes from design quality and substance, not fake social proof. If you include a "trusted by" / testimonial layout, fill it with clearly-marked placeholders.
- **Do NOT** overclaim (no "India's first AI construction company", no "replaces your team", no autonomous-AI promises).
- **Do NOT** use generic AI-SaaS aesthetics (purple gradients, glowing orbs, neon, robot/brain art).
- Any product screenshots = clean placeholder mocks with obvious slots to drop real screenshots in later.

## Build

Modern, fast, fully responsive (mobile-first), accessible to **WCAG 2.1 AA** (the product's own principle — colour is never the only signal; strong contrast on the warm palette). Tasteful, performant motion. Use a clean component system (React + Tailwind, or your best choice). Ship a polished, production-quality result — this is the company's front door.

Deliver: the full landing page + a pricing sub-page + a placeholder Site-Survey-tool sub-page, with a tidy design system (tokens for the palette/type above) so it's easy to extend.
