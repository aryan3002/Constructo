# Constructo Homeowner App — Fresh Redesign · Screen Plan (Stitch)

**Design language:** "Calm Cockpit" — Uber-grade glanceable clarity + warm editorial voice + a signature **living-home hero**. Warm Paper canvas, Calm Pine primary, Warm Clay celebration accent, Anek/Hind/Spline Mono, no percentage rings ever, evidence-on-tap, voice/photo-first, Hindi/Hinglish-first.

**Stitch project:** `Constructo — Homeowner (Fresh Redesign)` (id `14655651634697253072`)
**Design system asset:** `assets/ffb723eb8d164ae5a29a3851b56b74e1`

**Navigation:** 4-tab bottom bar — **Home · Photos · Updates · Design** — + floating "✨ Ask" pill on every tab. Settings/Members behind the Home top-bar avatar. No 5th tab.

---

## Batch 1 — The 4 core tabs (the spine)
1. **Home — On track** (flagship "am I okay?"): living-home hero, time-bar status card, shortcut rail, latest-from-site strip, weekly summary card. *[generating]*
2. **Home — Needs attention**: same shell, but a warn/risk Needs-Attention card surfaces one decision/delay with "+₹ · reversible · Review →".
3. **Home — Quiet period**: no recent photos → a calm grey (never red) explainer card ("Site quiet 3 days — curing, normal"). Proves the anti-silence behavior.
4. **Photos & Videos**: AI search bar, "Latest" hero strip, segmented [All · By Room · By Milestone · My visits], grouped grid with room chips, FAB "+". Captions in her language.
5. **Photo viewer (full-screen)**: big photo, bilingual caption, date + room + "✓ by site", ⓘ jargon-translate, Save / Share / Hide.
6. **Project Updates — Timeline**: pinned Weekly Summary card + feed of card archetypes (progress / milestone / delay / change / quiet). Sub-tabs: Timeline · Milestones · Changes · Property.
7. **Updates — Milestones**: vertical delivery-tracker with range estimates ("usually 10–18 days, day 8"), evidence packets on done items. No %.
8. **Updates — Changes**: cost-change story log (WHAT/WHY/+₹/+days/WHO approved), running total pinned.
9. **Design Documents (hub)**: Style Profile card, Plans & Drawings, Room-by-Room cards, Inspiration board, Monthly digest. The only "creator" tab.

## Batch 2 — Key flows & moments
10. **Weekly Summary detail**: the full warm "letter" — what got done / coming next / needs you / delays — 🔊 read-aloud, 📤 share family.
11. **Decision detail (the pre-brief)**: "A decision for you · 1 of 1", why-now, real option photos, what-each-means, taste cross-link (advisory), Choose A/B, "Ask first", "✓ Reversible until …".
12. **Flag an issue (capture-first)**: Step 1 big 📷 Photo / 🎙 hold-to-talk; Step 2 AI-drafted editable title + room + soft urgency chips + Send.
13. **My Requests tracker**: Open / Resolved, real reply threads, actor labels, SLA promise.
14. **Ask / Assistant ("Poochho")**: grounded chat — honesty banner, cited answers, abstain-and-handoff, text + 🎙 voice, suggested chips.
15. **Design Intake — Step 1**: "What feels like home?" real-photo preference grid + 🎙 voice escape hatch.
16. **Design Intake — Step 3**: "Here is your design vibe" — Hinglish profile, grounding badge, per-room chips, 🎙 adjust-by-voice, confirm.

## Batch 3 — Onboarding, household, settings, net-new
17. **Auth — phone + OTP** (join via deep-link): one door, +91, 6 mono OTP boxes.
18. **Welcome (invited)**: templated truth — "Welcome to Sharma Residence · invited by Verma Constructions as Primary Owner".
19. **Household setup**: add family members, roles (Primary / Co-owner / Family / Advisor), design-say.
20. **Settings hub**: Members · Notifications · Language · Privacy · Help, with live human subtitles.
21. **Members**: roster with named roles + capability lines + notif cadence; invited (half-avatar); graceful authority.
22. **My Notifications**: As-it-happens / Daily / Weekly / Pause + non-negotiable spikes always punch through.
23. **Handover + Snag-list** (net-new): graduation tabs Checklist · Walkthrough · Warranty; count bar (not %), photo-evidence snags.
24. **"This week in 60 seconds" recap** (net-new): stories-style player, real photos only, AI Hinglish text, share.
25. **Calm Notifications / Inbox**: partitioned NEEDS YOU vs GOOD TO KNOW; "All caught up" empty state.
