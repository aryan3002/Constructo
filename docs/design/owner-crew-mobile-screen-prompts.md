# Owner & Crew — MOBILE app screen prompts for Claude Design

Paste one at a time into the project with the owner/crew design system. Replace **"Blueprint"** with the system's name. These are the **mobile** field/phone screens (the surface being rebuilt) — they test whether the Blueprint soul (warm paper + ink + safety-amber + ledger numerals) works sized for a thumb. Field screens are **light** (paper reads in the sun); ask for a **dark** variant of the owner/PM glance for evening/indoor.

Shared reminder prepended to each: *Blueprint on mobile — warm-paper canvas, near-black ink, `#F2A100` safety-amber as the ONE primary, mono tabular ₹ (Indian grouping ₹1,20,000), status = colour + icon + word, evidence-on-tap, NEVER a %, ≥48px tap targets (≥72px for field capture), voice/photo before forms, offline-tolerant.*

## 1 · Supervisor — Field Capture (Hindi-first) — point-shoot-done

> Blueprint on mobile, **Hindi-first (Devanagari)**, on-site (glare, gloves, one hand). Design the **supervisor's Capture home**.
>
> **Top:** calm header — site name "Whitefield Villa", today's date, a small sync indicator ("✓ synced" / "⏳ queued").
> **Center (dominant):** TWO huge capture controls, each ≥72px — a big **amber hold-to-talk** mic ("बोलकर बताएं — दबाकर रखें") and a big **camera** ("फ़ोटो भेजें"); a tiny "टाइप करें" link beneath (typing last).
> **Below — a confirm card** from a just-spoken voice note: **"आज 12 मज़दूर आए · पहली मंज़िल की छत की ढलाई"**, extracted numbers as **mono chips** (मज़दूर · 12), a "✦ साइट से" note, and **[पुष्टि करें ✓] [बदलें]**.
> **Below:** today's quick log (2 calm rows) + a "समस्या बताएं" (raise issue) affordance.
> One obvious thing to do. Same paper/ink/amber/mono as the web, but sized for a thumb in the sun — nothing dense.

## 2 · Owner — "Needs you" on the phone — clear it in 60 seconds

> Blueprint on mobile, light. Design the **Owner's phone home — "Needs you"** — decisions-from-evidence on the go.
>
> **Header:** "Good morning" + company + a one-line portfolio pulse ("3 sites · 1 needs you · this week ₹4,20,000 in").
> **Body:** ≤3 **ranked exception cards** stacked worst-first, each a tap-to-expand brief — status pill (colour+icon+word), plain one-line claim, ₹ in mono, "show proof ▾", and a thumb-sized one-tap action row:
> - **RISK · Unverified invoice** — "ACC cement · 120 billed vs 100 logged · ₹72,000" · **[Approve ₹72,000] [Hold] [Assign]** (owner-only money). Show this card **expanded** → the timestamped challan photo + the invoice line inline.
> - **WARN · Labour shortfall** — "Whitefield Villa · 8 of 14 · day 3" · **[Assign →]**
> **Footer:** a calm "2 more, all good →" + a simple bottom nav (Home · Sites · Reconcile · More).
> A decision queue you clear standing in a doorway — never a dashboard. *Then render the **dark** variant (evening/indoor): lifted amber, deep-ink canvas, same legibility.*

---

## 3 · PM — "Today" (role-aware: propose, don't approve)

> Blueprint on mobile, light. Design the **PM's home — "Today"**.
>
> **Header:** PM name + "3 sites need a look".
> **Body:** per-site **brief command cards** (worst-first), each summarising that site's top exception with a **role-shaped** one-tap action:
> - money kinds (unverified invoice, pending approval) → PM sees **"Propose to owner →"** (NEVER "Approve" — money is owner-only)
> - operational kinds (labour shortfall, material delay) → **"Assign →"**
> Each card: site name, status pill, the claim + ₹ in mono, "show proof ▾".
> Plus one **"Send today's DPR"** card with a small **confidence meter** ("92% · ready") and a **[Review & send]** (amber) — AI drafted it, the PM sends (never auto-sends).
> Evidence-first, mono numerals, no %, calm density.

## 4 · Mukadam — the simplest possible capture (near-zero literacy)

> Blueprint on mobile, **Hindi**, light. Design the **mukadam** capture — for someone who barely uses a phone. The whole screen is ONE job:
> - site name small at top,
> - then ONE **giant amber "बोलें" (talk) button** filling most of the screen, with a tiny camera button beside it,
> - after speaking, a single big confirm card: **"12 मज़दूर · छत की ढलाई"** with **[✓ ठीक है] [बदलें]**.
> Nothing else — no nav clutter, no dense data, no menus. The simplest capture that still feels like the same product (paper, ink, amber, mono number). Big, calm, unmissable.

### What each tells you
- **#1 Supervisor Capture** — does the **field rebuild** land? Voice/photo-first, Hindi, big targets in the sun.
- **#2 Owner Needs-you** — does **decisions-from-evidence** work at phone scale (+ dark mode)?
- **#3 PM Today** — does the **role-aware** authority (propose vs approve) read clearly on mobile?
- **#4 Mukadam** — does it stretch to the **lowest-literacy** extreme without losing the brand?

If a screen feels off, tune: amber overused (should be rare — one primary), targets too small for gloves, density creeping in (cut rows, rank harder), or the field screens feeling too "calm/homeowner" (they should feel like a fast, confident tool — just bigger).
