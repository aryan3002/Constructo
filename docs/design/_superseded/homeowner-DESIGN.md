## Brand & Style

Constructo's homeowner app has one job: **reassure**. The owner is anxious — often non-resident, building a ₹50L–5Cr house through a builder they half-trust, in a language and process they only partly understand. The app's success is **earned absence**: she opens it, learns "you're okay — nothing needs you today," and closes it calm. An empty screen she trusts is the product working. We never measure engagement; we measure peace of mind.

The aesthetic is **"Calm Cockpit"** — the glanceable clarity of Uber fused with the warmth of a Sunday-evening letter from your builder, anchored by a signature **"living home"** hero photo that visibly grows as the house is built. It feels premium, human, and certain — never an enterprise dashboard, never a spreadsheet, never a construction-industry safety-orange tool. Think Apple Health's calm + Airbnb's warmth + Uber's "one clear answer, one clear action."

The whole product is organized around defusing **four anxieties**: **Silence** (no news → we explain the quiet), **Jargon** (a term she can't parse → we translate to her words), **Money** (an unexpected number → we tell the story before the amount, and show who approved it), and **Decision** (a choice at 9pm with no context → we pre-brief it and promise it's reversible).

**Tone:** calm, warm, plain-spoken, honest. Reads like a trusted person, not software. Never alarms without a next step. Never fakes certainty — when unsure, it says so and offers one tap to "ask your builder." Hindi/Hinglish first; her words, mirrored back.

## Colors

A warm, grounded, residential palette — earthy and domestic, never industrial.

- **Canvas — Warm Paper (`#FAF6EE`):** the app background. Soft and warm, never clinical white; lowers anxiety and makes white cards float.
- **Cards — Pure White (`#FFFFFF`):** content surfaces pop gently against the warm canvas.
- **Primary — Calm Pine (`#1E7A63`):** a deep, reassuring green-teal for primary actions, "on track" states, active navigation. Calm and confident — the opposite of alarm. This is the color of "everything is fine."
- **Secondary — Warm Clay (`#C5683B`):** an earthy terracotta reserved for **celebration, milestones, and the warmth of "this is becoming your home."** Used sparingly, for joy — never for warnings.
- **Status spine (a language she learns once — ALWAYS color + icon + word, NEVER color alone):**
  - On track / done / matched → **`#1E9E5A`** with a ✓ check.
  - Needs attention / pending → **`#E8A317`** with a ! / clock.
  - Risk / delay / money issue → **`#E5484D`** with a △. **Red is reserved for genuine delay/risk only** — never decorative, never on a quiet-period card, never on a taste choice.
  - Change / neutral update → **`#3B7DD8`** with an ⇄ / i.
- **Text:** `#1E2230` (primary), `#5B6166` (muted/metadata). Hairlines `#D9D2C2`.

## Typography

Multi-script, **Devanagari-first** — every typeface must render Hindi (Devanagari) and Latin beautifully at small sizes. This rules out Latin-only display serifs.

- **Display & headlines — Anek (Anek Latin + Anek Devanagari):** confident, warm, pan-Indian, variable. Set one notch larger and a touch lighter than a utility app would — editorial, breathing, calm. Used for greetings, the status sentence, milestone titles.
- **Body & UI — Hind:** engineered for Devanagari + Latin UI legibility; open, approachable, high x-height. Keeps construction detail from feeling technical.
- **Numerals, ₹ amounts, dates, durations — Spline Sans Mono:** tabular figures give a calm, "this is an exact record" feel and prevent numbers from jumping. **Always render money Indian-grouped with a leading ₹ and no space (`₹1,20,000`, never `₹120,000`).**

Type scale runs large and airy (Display 34 / Headline 28 / Title 18 / Body 16–18 / Label 14). **Never below 14px.** Hindi runs ~15–20% longer than English — use flexible containers, never fixed-width text.

## Layout & Spacing

A relaxed, single-column, **one-thing-per-glance** rhythm on a 4px grid with generous 20px side margins.

- **One answer above the fold.** The Home screen answers "am I okay?" in the top third — a living-home hero + a single status sentence — before anything else loads.
- **Vertical breathing room:** 24–32px between major sections so nothing feels cluttered or urgent.
- **Conditional cards:** cards that have nothing to say **disappear** (no "0 pending", no empty states pretending to be content). Empty is good news, said out loud ("All calm tonight — nothing needs you").
- **Touch targets ≥ 48×48px** everywhere (older owners, one-thumb, on the move). Primary actions sit in the bottom thumb-zone.
- **Navigation:** a clean **4-destination bottom bar** (Home · Photos · Updates · Design) — Uber-simple, icon + word, the active item in Calm Pine. A persistent floating **"Ask" pill** (✨) above the bar on every tab. Settings & household live behind a top-bar avatar/gear, never a 5th tab.

## Elevation & Depth

Soft, warm, residential depth — gentle, never floaty-techy.

- **Cards:** white on warm paper, lifted by a very diffuse, low-opacity warm shadow (`0 8px 24px rgba(60,50,30,0.06)`). They feel like photographs resting on a warm table.
- **The living-home hero:** full-bleed real photo with a soft bottom-up scrim so the greeting + status sentence stay legible over any image.
- **Press:** cards scale to ~98% with a slight shadow tighten — a calm, physical tap.
- **Sheets & modals:** rise from the bottom with a soft backdrop dim (light blur), 24px top radius.

## Shapes

Soft-geometric and friendly — rounding removes any industrial sharpness.

- **Cards & hero:** 16–20px radius (large, calm).
- **Sheets / bottom modals:** 24px top radius.
- **Chips, thumbnails, inputs:** 12px.
- **Buttons & the Ask pill:** fully pill-shaped (9999px) — maximally friendly and tappable.

## Motion

Calm and gentle (200–320ms, soft easing) — the opposite of urgent.

- Content fades and rises softly; the status sentence never animates in a way that delays reading it.
- **Milestone complete:** a small, warm "confetti-lite" in Warm Clay — once, dismissible. Used sparingly, for genuine joy.
- **Quiet states fade, never pulse** (pulsing reads as alarm).
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Components

### Living-Home Hero (signature)
A full-bleed **real, contractor-published photo** of the house, refreshed as the build progresses (the literal answer to "show me it's becoming a home"). Over it: a time-of-day greeting in her language ("सुप्रभात, Priya"), the property name, and a single **status chip** (icon + color + 1–3 words, e.g. "● On track · ~Nov 2026"). A ⚙/avatar button top-right. **Never** an AI-generated or 3D render — always a real photo a human took.

### Status Card with TIME-BAR (never a percentage)
The emotional core. A horizontal **time-bar** showing position between Start and Handover — `Started Jan'26 ●━━━━◇┄┄┄ ~Handover Nov'26` — with a warm "you-are-here" dot and faint ticks for completed milestones. **NO percentage ring, NO "63% done" — ever** (a frozen percentage manufactures the very anxiety we exist to remove; time always moves). Below the bar: the current milestone name and one plain-language **status sentence** in Hinglish ("छत की ढलाई पूरी, 14 दिन क्योरिंग — सामान्य।"), with a small "✦ Reviewed by site" trust badge.

### Needs-Attention Card (only when something genuinely needs her)
A single warn/risk-bordered card naming **one** thing to do, what it costs ("+₹0 · reversible"), and one clear action ("Review →"). One thing at a time. Absent when there's nothing — its absence is the reassurance.

### Evidence Card ("Show proof ▾")
Any claim, number, or update reveals its proof in one tap — a timestamped real photo, a challan, a voice note, the original message. Expands in place. This is the soul of trust: nothing is asserted that can't be shown.

### Shortcut Rail / Home Widgets
A compact, glanceable set of tappable tiles under the status card (NEXT UP · N PHOTOS · CHANGES ₹ · ASK 🎙) — the Uber-style "everything one tap away" without clutter. Tiles with nothing to say collapse.

### Photo Tile + Caption
Real photos with AI captions **in her language** (EN + HI), date, room tag, and a ⓘ jargon tap-to-translate. Save / Share / Hide actions. Hide is per-member and always reversible.

### Voice / Photo First Capture
For flagging an issue or asking a question, the default input is a big **📷 Photo** or **🎙 hold-to-talk** — typing is a small secondary link, never the first ask. Confirms optimistically; tolerant of poor connectivity.

### Decision Card
When she must choose, the card **pre-briefs**: why this matters now, real option photos, what each means in plain words, a cross-link to her taste profile (advisory, never blocking), clear choices, and "✓ Reversible until [date]." Never a bare "Approve?" with no context.

### Weekly Summary Card
A pinned, warm, letter-style recap ("THIS WEEK · 2–8 Jun") — what got done, what's next, anything needing her, any delays — AI-drafted, builder-reviewed, with a 🔊 read-aloud and 📤 share-with-family.

### Buttons & Chips
- **Primary:** Calm Pine pill, white text, ≥48px, icon + word.
- **Secondary:** transparent with a Pine outline.
- **Celebration / milestone accents:** Warm Clay.
- **Status chips:** soft tinted pill (status color at low opacity) + icon + word + high-contrast text.

### Floating "Ask" Pill (✨)
Persistent above the bottom bar on every tab — "कोई सवाल? Ask" — opens the grounded assistant (answers only from her project's published updates, cites its source, and when it can't be sure, abstains and offers to send the question to her builder).

## Accessibility & Field Reality (first-class, not optional)
- Contrast ≥ 4.5:1; **status = color + icon + word, never color alone.**
- **App language is user-selectable and SINGLE-LANGUAGE per screen — never two languages on one screen.** Two modes: (1) **English** — 100% English UI; (2) **Hindi** — Hindi/Devanagari UI, warm and natural, with English only where necessary (names, the brand "Constructo", unavoidable tokens like OTP/PDF/₹ and numbers). In either mode, show the user **her own words** back, never jargon. (Bilingual examples in this doc are source pairs — pick one side per the active mode.)
- **Voice-out (read-aloud)** available on summaries and confirmations for low-literacy or on-the-move use.
- Touch targets ≥ 48px; large, legible type (≥14px); flexible layouts for Hindi text expansion.
- Offline-tolerant; never a blocking "no internet" wall — show last-known, labeled.
- ₹ + lakh/crore Indian digit grouping for every amount.

## Anti-slop — do NOT
- ❌ A percentage / progress ring or "% complete" anywhere (use the time-bar).
- ❌ AI-generated or 3D house renders — only real photos a human took.
- ❌ Inter / Roboto / system-font defaults (use Anek + Hind).
- ❌ Purple→blue AI gradients; safety-orange / construction-yellow industrial styling.
- ❌ Spreadsheet-grid or dashboard homepages; 12-field forms.
- ❌ Icon-only critical buttons; color-only status.
- ❌ Red used decoratively; alarms without a next step; gamified streaks/badges.
- ❌ Clinical pure-white app background (use Warm Paper).
