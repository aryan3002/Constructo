**Avatar** — builder / person identity. Real photo or tidy initials on a tinted square.

```jsx
<Avatar name="Verma Constructions" />            {/* "VC" on clay squircle */}
<Avatar name="Priya Sharma" tone="green" shape="circle" size="sm" />
<Avatar name="Site Engineer" src="/uploads/eng.jpg" size="lg" />
```

Tones: `clay | green | neutral`. Builders read best as a `rounded` squircle; people as a `circle`.
