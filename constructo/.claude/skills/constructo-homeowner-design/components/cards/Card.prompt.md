**Card** — the base warm surface container.

```jsx
<Card>…</Card>
<Card variant="letter">…weekly summary…</Card>
<Card variant="quiet" elevated={false}>…calm empty state…</Card>
```

`variant`: `plain` (card surface) · `letter` (warm panel) · `quiet` (dashed, low-emphasis). `padding`, `elevated`, and `as` are configurable.
