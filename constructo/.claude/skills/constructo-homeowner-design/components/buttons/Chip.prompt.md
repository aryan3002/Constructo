**Chip** — a small pill for quick actions and lightweight toggles. `tone="accent"` marks the AI "Ask" affordance.

```jsx
<Chip tone="accent" icon="sparkles">Ask</Chip>
<Chip icon="calendar-check">Request a visit</Chip>
<Chip icon="flag">Flag something</Chip>
<Chip selected>EN</Chip>
```

Secondary control (~40px) — not for primary actions. Use `selected` for the active toggle state.
