**IconButton** — a single-icon control with a guaranteed ≥48px target. Always pass `label`.

```jsx
<IconButton icon="arrow-left" label="Back" />
<IconButton icon="camera" label="Take a photo" tone="accent" size="lg" />
<IconButton icon="mic" label="Hold to talk" tone="bare" />
```

Tones: `neutral` (sand surface) · `accent` (green tint) · `bare` (transparent).
